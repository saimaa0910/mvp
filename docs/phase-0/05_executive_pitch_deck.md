## Slide 2 — The Challenge

### 183 Namma Clinics. Zero Digital Records.

| What's happening today | Impact |
|---|---|
| 📋 All records on paper registers | Patient history unavailable across visits |
| 🚫 No real-time visibility for BBMP | Issues surface weeks late |
| 💊 Medicine stock-outs discovered too late | Patients turned away |
| 📝 Illegible handwritten prescriptions | Patient confusion, dosage errors |
| 📬 Paper referrals, no tracking | < 5% follow-through rate |
| 📊 Monthly reports take 2–3 days to compile | Wasted clinical hours |

**~395,000 patients/month are served without a single digital record.**

## Slide 4 — Our Solution

### End-to-End Digital Platform

```
Reception → Triage → Doctor EMR → Pharmacy → Lab → Referrals
                      ↕
              Analytics & Dashboards
              ↕
         ABDM / ABHA Integration
```

**7 core modules** covering the complete OPD journey:
1. 👤 Patient Registration & Queue (ABHA-ready)
2. 🩺 Nurse Triage (vitals + danger flags)
3. 💊 Doctor EMR (templates + bilingual Rx)
4. 🏥 Pharmacy (dispense + stock alerts)
5. 🔬 Lab (test tracking + results)
6. ↗ Referrals (digital slip + SMS tracking)
7. 📊 Analytics (clinic → zone → city dashboards)

## Slide 5 — Design Principles

| Principle | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Clinic-First** | Minimal typing. Quick-pick buttons. Condition templates. Works offline. |
| **Citizen-First** | Bilingual (Kannada + English) prescriptions. SMS reminders. Clear visit summaries. |
| **ABDM-Ready** | ABHA capture from day 1. Phased HIP/HIU integration. |
| **Data-Governed** | 100% government data ownership. Open APIs. No vendor lock-in. |
| **Outcome-Led** | Real-time dashboards. AI stock forecasts. Disease cluster alerts. |

## Slide 8 — Pilot Plan

### 20 Clinics Across All 5 Zones

- **4 clinics per zone** (North, South, East, West, Central)
- **Mix of volumes:** 7 high, 8 medium, 5 low footfall
- **Hardware provided:** Desktops, tablets, printers, UPS, 4G backup
- **Training:** In-person, role-specific, 4–6 hours per staff member (in Kannada)
- **Support:** On-site for first 5 days, then helpdesk (phone + WhatsApp)

### Pilot Success = Gateway to Citywide

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Digital registration adoption | ≥ 80% |
| Prescription digitization | ≥ 70% |
| System uptime | ≥ 99% |
| Dashboard data lag | < 5 minutes |
| Staff satisfaction | ≥ 3.5/5 |
