Respected Sir,

We are pleased to submit the **Detailed Project Report (DPR)** for the proposed **Namma Clinic Digital Health & Operations Platform** — a comprehensive, EMR-lite, ABDM-ready primary care digital solution designed to serve Bengaluru's 183+ operational Namma Clinics.

### Background

Following our initial discussions and the guidance received during the Discovery Workshop held on **28 August 2026** at BBMP Head Office, our team has conducted extensive field research spanning **25+ clinic visits across all 5 zones**, **8 stakeholder interviews** with BBMP officials and clinic staff, and a thorough **technical infrastructure assessment**. The findings and the proposed solution are captured in the enclosed DPR and supporting annexures.

### What This DPR Covers

1. **Field Research Report** — Clinic enumeration, footfall data, hardware audit, staffing model, referral pathway mapping (Annexure A)
2. **Workflow Mapping** — 12 clinical and operational workflows mapped from as-is to to-be (Annexure B)
3. **Technical Architecture** — Hosting, security, ABDM integration, offline resilience (Annexure C)
4. **Pilot Plan** — 20-clinic pilot across all 5 zones with success criteria (DPR Section 7)
5. **Financial Estimates** — Pilot: ₹1.65 Crore; Citywide: ₹12.80 Crore Year 1 (DPR Section 10)
6. **Data Privacy Annexure** — DPDP Act 2023 compliance, data governance framework (Annexure F)
7. **Commercial Term Sheet** — 20-clinic pilot scope, SLAs, payment milestones (Annexure E)

### Our Commitment

We recognize that this project is not merely a software deployment — it is an opportunity to **transform how 4.7 million annual patient encounters** are recorded, tracked, and governed. Our platform is designed with five principles: **Clinic-first** (minimal typing), **Citizen-first** (bilingual Kannada-English output), **ABDM-ready** (ABHA from day one), **Data-governed** (100% government-owned data), and **Outcome-led** (real-time dashboards for governance).

### Our Ask

We respectfully request:

1. **Review of the enclosed DPR** by your office and the Health Department (suggested review period: 2 weeks)
2. **Designation of a Nodal Officer** from BBMP for project coordination
3. **A 60-minute presentation slot** for our team to walk through the DPR and demonstrate the interactive prototype
4. **Approval to proceed** with the 20-clinic pilot upon satisfactory review

We are confident that within **90 days of approval**, we can deliver a live, measurable, and citizen-friendly digital health system across 20 Namma Clinics — one that sets the national benchmark for urban primary healthcare digitization.
