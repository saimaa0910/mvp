## Section A — Role & Context

1. What is your primary role at this clinic / office?
2. How long have you been in this position?
3. How many patients do you typically see/handle per day?
4. What are the busiest hours of the day?

## Section B — Current Workflow Pain Points

5. Walk me through a typical patient visit from arrival to departure. What steps are involved?
6. Where do you spend the most time? What feels slow or inefficient?
7. What are the top 3 frustrations in your daily work?
8. Are there tasks you currently do that you feel are unnecessary or duplicated?
9. How do you handle patient records today? (Paper register, any software, etc.)
10. How do you handle repeat patients? Can you find their previous records easily?

## Section C — Technology Readiness

11. Do you use a computer or smartphone regularly? (1=Never, 5=Daily)
12. Have you used any health software before? Which one?
13. What concerns do you have about switching from paper to digital?
14. If you had a computer or tablet for your work, what feature would you want most?
15. How comfortable are you with typing in English? In Kannada?

## Section D — Specific Role Questions

### For Doctors:
16. How do you currently write prescriptions? How long does each take?
17. Do you refer patients frequently? To which facilities?
18. Would pre-filled templates for common conditions (fever, HTN, diabetes) help? Which conditions do you see most?
19. How do you handle NCD (hypertension/diabetes) follow-up patients?

### For Nurses:
20. What vitals do you routinely check for every patient?
21. How do you communicate vital information to the doctor?
22. Do you flag abnormal vitals? How?
23. How do you handle immunization scheduling?

### For Pharmacists:
24. How do you manage medicine stock currently?
25. How often do you face stock-outs? Which medicines run out most?
26. How long does stock reconciliation take?
27. Do you track medicine expiry dates? How?

### For Lab Technicians:
28. What tests do you perform at this clinic?
29. How do you track sample collection and results?
30. How do you communicate results to the doctor?

### For Administrators / Zonal Officers:
31. How do you compile monthly reports? How long does it take?
32. What data would you most want in real-time?
33. How do you monitor clinic performance across your zone/city?

## Section E — Wish List

34. If you could change one thing about how this clinic works, what would it be?
35. What would make patients happier with their clinic experience?
36. Any other suggestions or concerns about digital transformation?
