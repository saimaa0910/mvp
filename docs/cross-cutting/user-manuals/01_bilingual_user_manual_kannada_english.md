## 2. Role 1: Receptionist / Registration Clerk (ನೋಂದಣಿ ಸಿಬ್ಬಂದಿ)

### 2.1 Registering a New Citizen Patient (ಹೊಸ ರೋಗಿಯ ನೋಂದಣಿ)
1. **Navigate to Registration:** Click **"Registration & Queue" (ನೋಂದಣಿ ಮತ್ತು ಸರತಿ)** in the left sidebar.
2. **Search First:** Enter the citizen's 10-digit mobile number in the search bar. If the patient has visited before, click **"Check In"** to avoid duplicates.
3. **New Patient Form:** If not found, click **"+ New Patient" (+ ಹೊಸ ರೋಗಿ)**.
   * **Full Name (ಪೂರ್ಣ ಹೆಸರು):** Enter name in English. The system automatically suggests Kannada transliteration.
   * **Mobile Number (ಮೊಬೈಲ್ ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ):** 10 digits (Mandatory).
   * **Age & Gender (ವಯಸ್ಸು ಮತ್ತು ಲಿಂಗ):** e.g., `52`, `Male`.
   * **Service Category (ಸೇವೆಯ ವಿಭಾಗ):** Select `General OPD`, `Fever`, `NCD Screening`, `MCH`, or `Immunization`.
   * **ABHA Number (ಐಚ್ಛಿಕ - Optional):** Enter 14-digit ABHA if available.
4. **Issue Token:** Click **"🎫 Register & Issue Token"**. A thermal token slip (e.g., `A008`) will print instantly. Direct the patient to the Nurse Triage desk.

## 3. Role 2: Staff Nurse / Triage Station (ಶುಶ್ರೂಷಕಿ / ನರ್ಸ್ ತಪಾಸಣಾ ಕೇಂದ್ರ)

### 3.1 Capturing Vital Signs & Chief Complaints (ಪ್ರಮುಖ ಲಕ್ಷಣಗಳ ದಾಖಲಾತಿ)
1. **Select Patient:** In the **"Triage / Nurse" (ಟ್ರಯೇಜ್ / ನರ್ಸ್)** module, click the next waiting token from the left queue list.
2. **Measure & Enter Vitals:**
   * **BP Systolic / Diastolic (ರಕ್ತದೊತ್ತಡ):** e.g., `120` / `80` mmHg.
   * **Pulse Rate (ನಾಡಿಮಿಡಿತ):** e.g., `76` bpm.
   * **Temperature (ತಾಪಮಾನ):** e.g., `37.0` °C (or digital reading).
   * **SpO₂ (ಆಮ್ಲಜನಕದ ಪ್ರಮಾಣ):** e.g., `98` %.
   * **Random Blood Sugar (ರಕ್ತದಲ್ಲಿನ ಸಕ್ಕರೆ ಅಂಶ):** For patients aged $\ge 30$ or with diabetes history.
3. **Chief Complaints (ಮುಖ್ಯ ತೊಂದರೆಗಳು):** Tap on complaint chips (`Fever`, `Cough`, `Headache`, `Body pain`, etc.).
4. **Automated Danger Flags (ತುರ್ತು ಎಚ್ಚರಿಕೆ):**
   * If BP $\ge 180/110$ or SpO₂ $< 90\%$, the screen flashes a **RED DANGER ALERT (🚨 ತುರ್ತು ಪರಿಸ್ಥಿತಿ)**.
   * The patient is automatically elevated to top priority in the Doctor's consultation queue.
5. **Save:** Tap **"✅ Save Vitals & Send to Doctor" (ವೈದ್ಯರಿಗೆ ಕಳುಹಿಸಿ)**.

## 4. Role 3: Medical Officer / Doctor EMR (ವೈದ್ಯರ ಸಮಾಲೋಚನೆ)

### 4.1 Conducting Digital Consultation (ಸಮಾಲೋಚನೆ ಮತ್ತು ಚಿಕಿತ್ಸೆ)
1. **Open Patient Record:** Go to **"Doctor EMR" (ವೈದ್ಯರ ಕನ್ಸೋಲ್)**. Click the active patient in your queue.
2. **Review Triage Data:** View previous visit history, allergies, and the vitals captured by the nurse.
3. **Clinical Notes & Templates (ಟಿಪ್ಪಣಿ ಮತ್ತು ಟೆಂಪ್ಲೇಟ್‌ಗಳು):**
   * Use 1-tap clinical templates: `Fever`, `Cough / Cold`, `Hypertension`, `Diabetes`, `Diarrhea`.
   * The template automatically populates standard physical exam findings and provisional diagnosis.
4. **Prescribe Medication (ಔಷಧಿ ಸೂಚನೆ):**
   * Search medication (e.g., `Paracetamol 500mg`, `Amlodipine 5mg`).
   * Select Dosage (`1 tablet`), Frequency (`Twice daily / ದಿನಕ್ಕೆ ಎರಡು ಬಾರಿ`), and Duration (`5 days`).
   * Instructions automatically generate in both English and Kannada on the printed prescription.
5. **Lab Orders & Referrals:** Add required diagnostic tests (`CBC`, `Dengue NS1`) or initiate a referral to Bowring/Victoria Hospital.
6. **Save & Print:** Click **"✅ Save Consultation & Send to Pharmacy"**. Click **"🖨️ Print Prescription"** to hand the bilingual slip to the patient.

## 5. Role 4: Pharmacist / Storekeeper (ಔಷಧ ತಜ್ಞರು / ಫಾರ್ಮಸಿ)

### 5.1 Dispensing Medications (ಔಷಧಿ ವಿತರಣೆ)
1. **Open Queue:** In **"Pharmacy & Dispensing" (ಫಾರ್ಮಸಿ)**, review the list of doctor-prescribed prescriptions.
2. **Check Stock:** The screen shows real-time available inventory for each prescribed item.
3. **Dispense:** Check the physical batch number, explain dosage instructions in Kannada to the patient, and click **"Dispense" (ವಿತರಿಸಿ)** for each item.
4. **Auto-Stock Update:** The inventory ledger automatically decrements the exact quantity. When all items are issued, the encounter marks as **"DONE" (ಪೂರ್ಣಗೊಂಡಿದೆ)**.

### 5.2 Managing Stock & Indents (ದಾಸ್ತಾನು ಮತ್ತು ಬೇಡಿಕೆ ಪಟ್ಟಿ)
* Review the **"Stock Alerts" (ದಾಸ್ತಾನು ಎಚ್ಚರಿಕೆ)** tab daily at 09:00 AM.
* Any medication with stock below the minimum threshold displays in **AMBER (ಕಡಿಮೆ ದಾಸ್ತಾನು)** or **RED (ಖಾಲಿಯಾಗಿದೆ)**.
* Click **"+ Indent" (+ ಬೇಡಿಕೆ ಪಟ್ಟಿ)** to auto-generate a replenishment request to the BBMP Central Drug Store.

## 6. Role 5: Laboratory Technician (ಪ್ರಯೋಗಾಲಯ ತಂತ್ರಜ್ಞರು)

1. **Pending Orders:** Open **"Lab & Diagnostics" (ಪ್ರಯೋಗಾಲಯ)** to view test orders generated by the Doctor.
2. **Sample Collection:** Collect blood/urine sample, tap **"→ Sample Collected" (ಮಾದರಿ ಸಂಗ್ರಹಿಸಲಾಗಿದೆ)**.
3. **Result Entry:** Once the test or rapid kit is processed, enter the numerical or qualitative result (`Negative`, `13.2 g/dL`).
4. **Verify & Complete:** Tap **"✅ Save Result" (ಫಲಿತಾಂಶ ದಾಖಲಿಸಿ)**. The result links to the patient's digital record immediately.

---

## 7. Role 6: Zonal Medical Officer & Admin (ವಲಯ ಆರೋಗ್ಯಾಧಿಕಾರಿಗಳು)

1. **Analytics Dashboard:** Navigate to **"Analytics & Reports" (ವಿಶ್ಲೇಷಣೆ ಮತ್ತು ವರದಿಗಳು)**.
2. **Zone Overview:** Compare daily footfall, stock-out days, and referral pendency across all clinics in your zone.
3. **Outbreak Alerts (ಸಾಂಕ್ರಾಮಿಕ ಎಚ್ಚರಿಕೆ):** Monitor fever spike alerts to mobilize local malaria/dengue vector control teams.
4. **Automated Reports:** Click **"📥 Export Report"** on the 1st of every month to download the completed BBMP monthly MIS report.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ & ಪರಿಹಾರಗಳು)

* **Q: What if the internet goes down? (ಇಂಟರ್ನೆಟ್ ಸಂಪರ್ಕ ಕಡಿತಗೊಂಡರೆ ಏನು ಮಾಡುವುದು?)**
  * **A:** Do not stop working. The platform operates seamlessly offline in your browser. All visits and prescriptions are saved locally on the computer and sync automatically when internet restores.
* **Q: What if a power outage occurs? (ವಿದ್ಯುತ್ ವ್ಯತ್ಯಯವಾದರೆ?)**
  * **A:** The 1KVA UPS provides 30–45 minutes of power. All notes auto-save every 15 seconds.
* **Q: Helpdesk Support Contact (ಸಹಾಯವಾಣಿ ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ):**
  * **Toll-Free Helpline:** `1800-XXX-XXXX` (Monday–Saturday, 08:30 AM – 05:30 PM).
  * **WhatsApp Support:** `+91-98XXXXXXXX` (Instant assistance in Kannada & English).
