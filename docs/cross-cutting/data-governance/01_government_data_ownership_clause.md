# ⚖️ Sovereign Data Ownership & Contractual IP Safeguards
## Namma Clinic Digital Health & Operations Platform
### Legal & Governance Covenant between GBA / BBMP and K Mati
### Document Code: DG-OWN-01 | Classification: Legal & Governance Baseline | Date: September 2026

---

## 1. Statutory Preamble & Ownership Assertion

This Covenant sets forth the definitive, irrevocable, and non-negotiable legal principles governing data ownership, intellectual property boundaries, data sovereignty, and exit obligations for the **Namma Clinic Digital Health & Operations Platform**, implemented by **Kushagramati Analytics Pvt Ltd (K Mati)** for the **Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) / Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP) Health Department**.

> **Definitive Legal Declaration:**
> **All data—including but not limited to citizen demographic profiles, clinical encounter notes, vital parameters, diagnostic orders and results, prescriptions, drug dispensing records, pharmacy stock ledgers, referral logs, telemetry, and audit trails—originating from, processed through, or stored within the Namma Clinic Platform is and shall at all times remain the sole, absolute, and unencumbered property of the Greater Bengaluru Authority / BBMP Health Department (Government of Karnataka).**

---

## 2. Comprehensive Contractual Clauses

### Clause 1.1: Absolute Sovereign Data Ownership
1. The Government (GBA / BBMP) is the exclusive **Data Owner** and **Data Fiduciary** under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).
2. K Mati acts strictly and solely as a **Data Processor** providing technological and managed services under the direction of the Data Fiduciary.
3. Under no circumstances shall K Mati, its parent entities, subsidiaries, subcontractors, technology partners, financiers, or assignees claim any proprietary interest, lien, copyright, patent right, commercial claim, or moral right over any dataset created or ingested in connection with this platform.

### Clause 1.2: Strict Data Monetization Prohibition
1. K Mati is explicitly and perpetually prohibited from selling, leasing, monetizing, licensing, sublicensing, publishing, trading, or commercializing any data, whether raw, processed, aggregated, pseudonymized, de-identified, or anonymized.
2. Any utilization of clinical or operational data for training external artificial intelligence models, commercial machine learning engines, or publishing research papers without the explicit, written prior sanction of the Special Commissioner (BBMP) constitutes a material breach of contract, invoking immediate termination for cause and statutory penalties under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and DPDP Act, 2023.

### Clause 1.3: Data Sovereignty & Geographic Residency
1. All active databases, backups, disaster recovery replicas, search indices, caches, and log archives must physically reside exclusively within the sovereign territory of the Republic of India at all times.
2. Hosting infrastructure shall be restricted to MeitY-empanelled Tier-III / Tier-IV Data Centers, the Karnataka State Data Centre (KSDC), or the National Informatics Centre (NIC) MeghRaj Cloud.
3. Cross-border data transfer, replication, or remote administrative access from outside India is strictly prohibited.

### Clause 1.4: Intellectual Property & Custom Module Handover
1. **Platform Core Framework:** Pre-existing background intellectual property, foundational framework libraries, and reusable data engineering pipelines developed by K Mati prior to contract execution remain K Mati IP, licensed to GBA / BBMP on a perpetual, royalty-free, irrevocable, enterprise-wide basis for municipal health operations.
2. **Custom Software Developments:** All custom schemas, integration adapters (specifically developed for BBMP, eHospital, KSDLPS, or state portals), dashboard templates, and localization modules funded through project milestones shall become the joint/exclusive property of GBA / BBMP.
3. **Source Code Escrow:** For long-term continuity, K Mati shall deposit the complete documented source code, build scripts, configuration files, and database schemas with a certified, government-approved Escrow Agent (or the Karnataka State e-Governance Agency). The release condition triggers automatically in the event of vendor insolvency, abandonment, or unrectified chronic breach of SLA.

### Clause 1.5: Data Portability & Vendor Lock-in Elimination
1. The platform must be architected with open standards, standard database schemas (PostgreSQL / ANSI SQL), and open API specifications (OpenAPI 3.0, FHIR R4).
2. The Government reserves the unilateral right to demand a complete, unencrypted, structured database dump (SQL + JSON + binary attachments) at any time.
3. Data exports must be delivered within **5 business days** of formal notice without requiring proprietary software tools, decoders, or licensable middleware to read or migrate.

### Clause 1.6: Termination, Data Handover & Sanitization Protocols
Upon expiry, completion, or premature termination of the contract:
1. **Transition Period:** K Mati shall provide a mandatory **90-day transition assistance period** to transfer operational knowledge and software support to the Government's designated internal IT team or successor vendor.
2. **Data Handover:** Deliver two certified complete cryptographic backups of all operational databases, analytics stores, and audit logs on physical encrypted enterprise media to the Special Commissioner's office.
3. **Cryptographic Erasure & Sanitization:** Following formal written receipt and verification of data handover by the Government, K Mati shall perform certified cryptographic sanitization (in compliance with NIST SP 800-88 Rev 1 standards) of all residual data from its staging, testing, and operational environments, submitting a signed Certificate of Data Sanitization within 14 calendar days.
